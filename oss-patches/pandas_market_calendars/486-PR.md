# CME: treat 2025-01-09 (Carter day of mourning) as an early close, not a holiday

Fixes #486

## Problem

Since 5.3.1.2, `CME_Agriculture` drops 2025-01-09 from the schedule entirely. CME didn't close that day. Its announcement (Dec 30, 2024) says:

> Interest rates and agricultural markets will close at 12:15 p.m. CT on Thursday, January 9.
>
> U.S. equity markets will be open until 8:30 a.m. CT on January 9.

Source: https://investor.cmegroup.com/news-releases/news-release-details/cme-group-announces-trading-hours-us-national-day-mourning-honor

`CME_Bond` and `CME_Equity` have the same problem: both drop the day instead of showing the early close.

## Root cause

c965d08 added 2025-01-09 to the shared `USNationalDaysofMourning` list in `holidays/us.py`. That was correct for NYSE and CBOE, which did close. But the CME calendars also use that list as `adhoc_holidays`, so they started treating the date as a full closure.

## Fix

In `calendars/cme.py`:

- Add `CMENationalDaysofMourning`, which is `USNationalDaysofMourning` without 2025-01-09. `CME_Agriculture`, `CME_Bond` and `CME_Equity` now use it for their ad hoc holidays.
- Add 2025-01-09 as a `special_closes_adhoc` entry with the published times: 12:15 CT for agriculture and rates, 08:30 CT for equity.

The shared US list is unchanged, so NYSE, CBOE and ICE behave exactly as before. I left `CME_TradeDate` alone. It already carries a `FIXME: unverified` note, and the press release doesn't talk about trade dates.
